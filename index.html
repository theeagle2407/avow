Avow enforcement protocol

Recurring payments should prove permission.

Avow is a verification and enforcement layer for subscription platforms, marketplaces and onchain payment systems. Before a renewal settles, it checks notice delivery, exact-price consent and current customer state—then makes the result enforceable.

The integration boundary

A production adopter connects four systems. After that, renewal checks can run automatically; end customers do not operate this console.

1
Renewal source

Send the subscription ID, current amount, proposed amount, renewal date and customer-state version.

2
Notice provider

Supply independently retrievable delivery evidence rather than a merchant-controlled checkbox.

3
Consent state

Record exact-price approval, cancellation and customer-state updates from the platform’s customer flow.

4
Settlement contract

Require a current CRE EXECUTE receipt before the recurring payment can be released.
