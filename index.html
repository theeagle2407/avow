How Avow works

A legal control between a proposed renewal and settlement.

Evidence becomes execution.

Avow does not merely flag a questionable payment. It verifies the facts, records the decision and makes that decision enforceable.

01

Read renewal

Load the proposed amount and customer state from Sepolia.

02

Verify notice

Retrieve provider-backed delivery evidence without exposing the recipient.

03

Apply control

CRE checks timing, cancellation and exact-price approval.

04

Enforce result

The onchain decision controls whether escrow can settle.
